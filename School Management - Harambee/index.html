Overview

Harambee Primary School is a well-established public, mixed day primary school located in the Makadara Constituency of Nairobi, Kenya.

Serving the Maringo/Hamza and Buruburu communities, the school provides accessible, foundational education from Early Childhood Development Education (ECDE) through primary levels for both boys and girls.

School Type

Public, Mixed Day School (Boys & Girls)

Levels Offered

ECDE (Nursery) & Primary Education

Enrollment

Over 1,100 students across 24 classrooms
